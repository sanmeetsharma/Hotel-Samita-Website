01 / Settle into the mountains

A day in the hills.
A room to come home to.

Head out for a day around Manali, then settle into a room with warm wooden interiors and views of the hills. There are four suite types, so you can choose the space that suits your trip.

Coming with family? Our Family Suite has two connected rooms. Prefer time outside? The Deluxe Suite has a private balcony for a morning tea or a quiet evening.

04 / Before you set off

Planning
your stay.

We have Hill View, Family, Deluxe, and Premium suites. Contact the hotel with your dates to check rates and availability.

The Family Suite has two connected rooms, so you can stay together with separate sleeping areas.

The Deluxe Suite has a private balcony. Let us know what you are looking for when you enquire, and we can help you choose a room.

Wi-Fi, parking, a lift, and power backup are available at the hotel. For check-in times or other questions about your stay, please contact us.
