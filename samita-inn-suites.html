ABOUT THE PROPERTY

The Samita Inn & Suites

Designed to feel like your ultimate home away from home, The Samita Inn & Suites offers expansive, fully-featured layouts perfect for extended stays or family getaways. The property features cozy bedrooms with traditional wooden setups and unobstructed mountain views, flowing into large living spaces centered around warm fireplaces. Guests can enjoy exclusive access to private balconies with outdoor seating, as well as a fully equipped kitchen and dining area for home-cooked meals. This homely charm is backed by robust infrastructure—including high-speed fiber internet, 24/7 security, private parking, and full generator backup—ensuring every modern convenience is at your fingertips.
