ABOUT THE PROPERTY

The New Samita Grand

The New Samita Grand elevates the luxury experience with its collection of contemporary suites. Whether you choose the Deluxe, Triplet, or the top-floor Premium Suite, you will be immersed in sophisticated design featuring modern wooden finishing, premium fixtures, and a touch of authentic antique decor. Every suite offers an expansive private balcony perfect for taking in the fresh mountain air. Your comfort is guaranteed with advanced climate control systems, alongside world-class property infrastructure including high-speed elevators, 24/7 security, dedicated fiber internet, private parking, and 100% generator backup for an uninterrupted getaway.
