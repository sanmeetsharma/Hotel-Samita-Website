Planning Your Stay

A few useful things to know before you book.

We have three hotels in Manali, with a choice of rooms, suites, and cottage spaces. Whether you are travelling as a couple, with friends, or with family, you can choose the stay that fits your trip and use it as a base for exploring the mountains.

The Family Suite at The Samita Grand has two connected rooms. The Samita Inn & Suites has bedrooms and living spaces for time together. Tell us how many people are travelling, including children, and we can help you check the room options.

Take a look at the Deluxe and Premium suites at The Samita Grand and The New Samita Grand for balcony and mountain-view options. Contact the hotel to check the room details and availability for your dates.

Contact the hotel you plan to stay at for meal options and timings. If you are booking a package, ask which meals are included so you know what to expect before you arrive.

Please tell the hotel about any dietary needs or food allergies before you book. The team can check what the kitchen is able to prepare and discuss the options with you.

Call or email us using the details below, or use the WhatsApp button on this page. Share your travel dates, the number of guests, and your preferred hotel so we can check availability.

Contact Us

The reservations team can confirm the payment methods available for your booking, any advance payment needed, and when the balance is due. Please check these details when confirming your stay.

Get in touch with your dates, group size, and any meeting or dining needs. We can check room availability and discuss which hotel and arrangements would suit your group.

If you are planning a birthday, anniversary, or festive break, tell us what you have in mind when you enquire. The hotel can confirm which arrangements and packages are available for your dates.

Contact us for the current rates and any offers for your travel dates. If you have stayed with us before, let the reservations team know when you get in touch.

The Samita Grand, The New Samita Grand, and The Samita Inn & Suites are in Manali, Himachal Pradesh. The Samita Resort and The Samita Villa in Kasol are under construction and are not open for stays yet.
